# Disclaimer

**Rehabit is not a medical device.** It performs no diagnosis, provides no treatment
recommendation, and is not a substitute for assessment or advice from a qualified healthcare
professional.

## What it is

A self-tracking notebook. It records the home exercise program someone was given by their own
therapist, and it estimates joint angles from a webcam so they can watch their own movement change
over weeks.

## What the measurements are, and are not

The angle figures produced by this software are **uncalibrated estimates derived from consumer
webcam imagery**, computed from a pose-estimation model on a two-dimensional image, in an
uncontrolled home environment, with no clinician present.

They are affected by camera placement, clothing, lighting, and any movement toward or away from the
lens. **The error can be substantial.** They are not goniometry. They have not been validated
against any clinical standard.

What they can reasonably show is the **direction of change in one person over time**, with the
camera in roughly the same place between sessions. They should not be used:

- to make or inform a clinical decision
- to judge whether a condition is improving or worsening
- to decide which exercises to do, or how hard to push
- to compare one person against another, or against a normative range

## Exercise content

The built-in exercise library exists so users can find the exercise they were already prescribed
without typing it. **It is not a recommendation.** Nothing in it was selected for any individual or
any condition, and the software deliberately makes no suggestion about sets, reps, frequency, or
progression — that information is entered by the user, from their own clinician.

## Safety

New or worsening pain, numbness, or weakness during exercise means stop and contact a clinician.

**Sudden face drooping, arm weakness, or speech difficulty are signs of a stroke and are a medical
emergency — call emergency services immediately.**
